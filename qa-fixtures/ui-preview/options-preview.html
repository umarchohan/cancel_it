Filtering style

What should happen when Cancel It finds something on your list?

Blur it

Softly blur the matching words or images. The rest of the page stays put.

Hide it

Remove matching content completely, as if it was never there.

Swap in a placeholder

Replace matches with a calm "Blocked" card so you know something was there.

Blur the whole page

If a page mentions anything on your list, blur the entire page. The strictest option.
