Cancel It Privacy Summary

Cancel It stores your blocked terms in Chrome storage so the extension can apply your settings across pages in your browser profile.

What the extension accesses

  • Visible page text and image metadata on pages you visit
  • Your blocked term list and filtering mode in Chrome storage

What the extension does not do

  • Does not send blocked terms or page content to an external server
  • Does not create user accounts
  • Does not sell personal data
  • Does not use advertising or analytics SDKs

Data retention

Your settings remain in your Chrome profile until you edit them, clear extension storage, or uninstall the extension.

Important note for store listing

Chrome Web Store publication still requires a public privacy policy URL. This bundled page is useful as source content, but you should also host the same policy on your website or a public document URL before submission.
