Cancel It — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Cancel It is a Chrome extension that hides, blurs, or replaces words and topics you choose on the webpages you visit. This policy explains exactly what data the extension accesses, what it stores, and what — if anything — ever leaves your device.

Data the extension reads

To find your chosen words on a page, Cancel It's content script reads the visible text and image metadata (alt text, titles) of pages you visit. This scanning happens entirely inside your browser. Page content is never transmitted anywhere, stored outside your browser, or seen by us.

Explicit-image detection (opt-in, off by default)

Settings → Filtering style has a switch called "Detect explicit images automatically." It is off by default. If you turn it on, Cancel It checks the pictures on the pages you visit and blurs the ones a small AI model judges to be sexually explicit. Blurred pictures stay blurred.

  • The model runs on your device. It ships inside the extension package and runs in your browser using your graphics chip. No image, image address, or model result is ever sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else.
  • Pictures are downloaded a second time. To check a picture, the extension requests it again from the website that hosts it (without your cookies and with the browser cache switched off), analyses it in memory, and discards it; no copy is saved. Pictures embedded directly in a page are analysed without a second download. The result is kept only in memory for as long as the browser needs it and is never written to storage. If you allow Cancel It in Incognito windows, it behaves the same way there.
  • Only counts are kept. If a picture is blurred, the local activity counters go up by one (shown in Activity as "explicit image"); the picture and its web address are not recorded.
  • It can be wrong. Some ordinary pictures may be blurred and some explicit ones may not be. Pictures that can't be downloaded or checked in time are shown normally.

Data the extension stores

The following is stored only in your Chrome browser profile (via chrome.storage), synced by Chrome across your own signed-in devices the same way your bookmarks are — never sent to our servers:

  • Your blocklist terms, enabled keyword packs, and custom packs you create
  • Per-site filtering rules and your chosen filtering style
  • Your Focus Mode list of sites to block and when the current focus session ends (Focus Mode only compares the site you're on with that list, on your device)
  • A salted hash of your optional parental-lock PIN (never the PIN itself)
  • Local counters of how much content has been filtered, and a custom placeholder image if you set one

Anonymous usage statistics (opt-in, off by default)

Settings → Support has a toggle called "Share anonymous usage stats." It is off by default. If you turn it on, Cancel It sends anonymous feature-usage events to Google Analytics (GA4) so we can see which features are actually used and improve the built-in keyword packs. This is the only way any data ever leaves your device.

These events are tied to a random identifier generated on your device — not your Google account, email, or any other identity. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn the toggle off at any time to stop future collection; past events already sent to Google Analytics are retained under Google's standard retention settings. To request deletion of previously shared data, contact us using the details below.

Keyword packs and custom pack names are user-entered text, so if you type something personal into a pack name or keyword while this toggle is on, that text is included. If you'd rather it never be sent, leave the toggle off — the extension works identically either way.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising
  • We do not require or support creating an account
  • We do not use remote-loaded or third-party tracking SDKs — analytics, when enabled, is a direct network call you can inspect in your browser's developer tools
  • We do not access your browsing history, passwords, or content on pages beyond what's needed to apply your own filtering rules

Permissions

Cancel It requests storage to save your settings, activeTab so the toolbar popup can show which site you're on, and host permissions on all sites because filtering only works on a page the content script can read (and, if you turn on explicit-image detection, so the extension can download a picture again to check it). It also requests offscreen, to run the image model in a hidden page inside the browser, and scripting, to keep pictures from flashing on screen before they've been checked. Those two are used only while explicit-image detection is on. None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data beyond what's described above.

Data retention and deletion

All locally stored data (blocklist, settings, stats) is deleted automatically when you uninstall the extension, or immediately if you clear it yourself from the Activity section or your browser's extension storage settings.

Children's privacy

Cancel It includes an optional "Family controls" PIN feature intended to help households share a device, but the extension does not knowingly collect data from children and does not distinguish user ages.

Changes to this policy

If what Cancel It collects changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that.
